# Tecer Educacional - Design System

## STEP 1 — VISUAL & BRAND ANALYSIS

### Brand Personality
The brand presents itself as **professional, empathetic, experienced, and transformative**. It acts as a guiding light for educational leaders, offering strategic clarity rather than just operational fixes. The tone is sophisticated yet deeply understanding of the daily struggles of school directors.

### Design Style
- **Modern & Institutional:** Clean layouts, ample whitespace, and structured typography that conveys authority and organization.
- **Elegant:** The use of serif headings and gold accents gives the brand a premium, high-value feel.
- **Minimal & Focused:** Interfaces are uncluttered, focusing entirely on the message and the strategic value provided.

### Emotional Tone
- **Trustworthy & Calming:** The deep green/blue backgrounds paired with soft beige and gold accents create a sense of stability, safety, and expertise.
- **Reassuring & Structured:** The messaging ("Tecer é transformar fios soltos em algo que faz sentido") combined with orderly grid layouts reassures users that their chaos can be organized.

### Target Audience
- **School Directors & Administrators:** Primarily female leaders (implied by "diretoras" and "juntas") in the private education sector who feel overwhelmed by operational tasks and seek strategic growth, financial health, and peace of mind.

### Perceived Trust and Authority Signals
- **Typography:** The elegant serif font (`Playfair Display`) instantly communicates heritage, intellect, and authority.
- **Color Palette:** Deep, rich backgrounds with gold highlights are classic indicators of premium quality and high-level consulting.
- **Direct Messaging:** Acknowledging the "solitude of leadership" shows deep empathy and insider knowledge.
- **Results-Oriented Content:** Clear pillars, "Before/After" comparisons, and specific outcomes (e.g., "Clareza nas decisões", "Paz para liderar").

### Visual Strategy
The visual strategy mirrors the brand's core promise: transforming chaos into clarity. The dark, structured sections represent the solid foundation of the Tecer Method, while the gold accents and lighter sections represent the "light," clarity, and premium outcomes achieved by the clients. The generous spacing gives the content "room to breathe," reflecting the "peace to lead" that the consultancy promises.

## STEP 2 — COLOR SYSTEM

The color palette is designed to convey trust, premium quality, and clarity. The contrast between deep background colors and luminous gold/beige accents creates a sophisticated and readable interface.

- **Primary 700 (Hover/Subtle)** — `#262e40` — `rgb(38, 46, 64)`
  - *Usage:* Subtle borders or hover states on dark backgrounds.

Warm, gold, and earthy tones used to draw attention, signify premium value, and highlight key actions.

- **Accent 500 (Gold/Action)** — `#d49a35` — `rgb(212, 154, 53)`
  - *Usage:* Primary call-to-action buttons, gradients, and active states.
- **Accent 400 (Gold Hover)** — `#a56e1d` — `rgb(165, 110, 29)`

- **Accent 100 (Beige/Sand)** — `#faf8f5` — `rgb(250, 248, 245)`
  - *Usage:* Primary text color on dark backgrounds, conveying a softer contrast than pure white.

### Neutral Colors
Grayscale tones for typography, borders, and light section backgrounds.

- **Neutral 900 (Dark Text)** — `#1f232e` — `rgb(31, 35, 46)`

## STEP 3 — TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

Typography plays a central role in conveying authority, elegance, and readability. The system pairs a classic serif for impactful headings with a clean sans-serif for functional reading.

### Font Families
- **Heading Font:** `Playfair Display`, serif.
  - *Usage:* H1-H6, large quotes, hero text, and significant numerical data.
- **Body & UI Font:** `Source Sans 3`, sans-serif.
  - *Usage:* Body paragraphs, captions, buttons, overlines, forms, and general UI elements.

### Heading Scale
Designed for high impact and clear hierarchy, line heights are kept tight for large text.

- **H1 (Display/Hero)**
  - *font-size:* `72px`
  - *line-height:* `72px` (1.0)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Main page title or hero section headline.
- **H2 (Section Heading)**
  - *font-size:* `48px`
  - *line-height:* `48px` (1.0)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Main title for sections (e.g., "Resultados", "Desafios").
- **H3 (Subsection/Card Heading)**
  - *font-size:* `24px` to `30px`
  - *line-height:* `32px` to `36px` (~1.3)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Titles within cards, pillars, or smaller section breaks.
- **H4 (Small Heading)**
  - *font-size:* `20px`
  - *line-height:* `28px` (1.4)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Sub-items, lists, or secondary card titles.
- **H5 & H6 (Component Level)**
  - *font-size:* `18px`
  - *line-height:* `24px` (1.33)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Small UI components or nested titles.

### Body Text Styles
Optimized for readability across long-form content.

- **Body Large (Intro/Lead)**
  - *font-size:* `20px`
  - *line-height:* `28px` (1.4)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Introductory paragraphs, hero subtext, and important summaries.
- **Body Default (Paragraph)**
  - *font-size:* `16px`
  - *line-height:* `24px` (1.5)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Standard paragraph text, descriptions, and FAQs.

### Caption Styles
- **Overline / Supertitle**
  - *font-size:* `14px` or `16px`
  - *line-height:* `24px` (1.5)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *text-transform:* `uppercase`
  - *letter-spacing:* `0.2em`
  - *Usage:* Section labels (e.g., "Próximo Passo", "Dúvidas Frequentes") placed above main headings.
- **Caption Small**
  - *font-size:* `12px` to `14px`
  - *line-height:* `16px` to `20px` (1.33 - 1.4)
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Footnotes, legal text, or secondary labels.

### Button Text Styles
- **Primary / Call-to-Action**
  - *font-size:* `18px` to `20px`
  - *line-height:* `24px` to `28px`
  - *font-weight:* `600` or `700` (Semibold/Bold)
  - *Usage:* Main action buttons (e.g., "Solicitar Diagnóstico Gratuito").
- **Secondary / Navigation**
  - *font-size:* `14px`
  - *line-height:* `20px`
  - *font-weight:* `400` (Regular)
  - *Usage:* Top navigation links and secondary actions.

## STEP 4 — SPACING & GRID

The layout uses generous spacing to emphasize clarity, focus, and a premium consulting feel. The grid system ensures content is well-aligned across devices.

### Spacing Scale
A consistent spacing system based on a 4px/8px baseline increment.

- **4px** (`1`): Minimal spacing (e.g., between icon and text).
- **8px** (`2`): Small internal padding, input spacing.
- **16px** (`4`): Standard component padding, gap between small items.
- **24px** (`6`): Component margins, card padding.
- **32px** (`8`): Gap between columns, spacing below headings.
- **48px** (`12`): Large gaps between related content blocks.
- **64px** (`16`): Margins between major sections on mobile/tablet.
- **96px** (`24`): Primary vertical padding for major page sections on desktop.

### Grid System
- **Columns:** 12-column fluid grid.
- **Gutter:** `24px` (`gap-6`) standard, scaling to `16px` (`gap-4`) on mobile.
- **Behavior:**
  - Desktop (`lg`): 3 or 4 columns (e.g., 3 columns for metrics or features).
  - Tablet (`md`): 2 columns.
  - Mobile (`sm`): 1 column.

### Layout Dimensions
- **Container Width (Max Width):**
  - Standard Section: `1152px` (`max-w-6xl`)
  - Narrow Content (e.g., FAQs, single column copy): `896px` (`max-w-4xl`)
- **Margins & Paddings:**
  - Standard Section Padding: `py-24` (96px top/bottom), `px-6` (24px left/right).
  - Mobile Section Padding: `py-16` (64px top/bottom), `px-4` (16px left/right).

### Responsive Breakpoints
Based on standard Tailwind CSS breakpoints:
- **Mobile (sm):** `>= 640px` (Stack layouts horizontally, adjust padding).
- **Tablet (md):** `>= 768px` (Switch from 1 column to 2 columns).
- **Desktop (lg):** `>= 1024px` (Full layout, multi-column).
- **Large Desktop (xl):** `>= 1280px` (Max container width applied).

## STEP 5 — UI COMPONENTS

The components emphasize clarity, premium aesthetics, and ease of interaction.

### Buttons
Buttons use a bold, confident typography style with subtle but premium interaction effects.

- **Primary Call to Action**
  - *Structure:* `bg-gradient-gold` (e.g., `#d49a35` to `#e6cc99`), rounded corners (`12px` or `xl`), prominent horizontal padding (`px-12`, `py-5`).
  - *States:*
    - **Hover:** Opacity 90% (`hover:opacity-90`), scale up slightly (`hover:scale-105`), subtle shadow (`shadow-gold`).
    - **Active:** Scale down to original size, slightly darker background.
    - **Disabled:** Background muted, opacity 50%, unclickable.
  - *Usage:* Main conversion points ("Solicitar Diagnóstico Gratuito").

- **Secondary Outline Button**
  - *Structure:* Transparent background, gold/light border (`border-gold-light/30`), white or light text.
  - *States:*
    - **Hover:** Subtle background fill (`hover:bg-primary-foreground/5`).
  - *Usage:* Secondary actions or alternative routes.

- **Navigation / Text Button**
  - *Structure:* Transparent background, small sans-serif text (`14px`), tracking wide.
  - *States:*
    - **Hover:** Text color change to brighter white/gold (`hover:text-primary-foreground`).
  - *Usage:* Header navigation and footer links.

### Cards & Accordions (FAQs)
Used to organize content clearly without overwhelming the user.

- **FAQ Accordion**
  - *Structure:* Light background (`bg-card`), rounded corners (`12px` or `xl`), thin border (`border-border`).
  - *Padding:* `24px` (`p-6`).
  - *Typography:* Serif heading (`H4` equivalent) for the question, sans-serif body for the answer.
  - *Iconography:* Chevron arrow on the right (`lucide-chevron-down`).
  - *States:*
    - **Hover:** Border color transitions to subtle gold (`hover:border-gold/30`).
    - **Active/Expanded:** Chevron rotates 180 degrees, answer block expands smoothly via max-height transition.

- **Feature / Pillar Cards**
  - *Structure:* Can be bordered or borderless (with background gradients). Usually arranged in a grid (`gap-6`).
  - *Typography:* Serif heading (`H3`), muted sans-serif body text.
  - *Spacing:* generous internal padding (`32px` or `p-8`).

### Navigation Bar
- *Structure:* Fixed or absolute positioning at the top, dark or transparent background.
- *Layout:* Logo on the left, navigation links centered, CTA button on the right (desktop).
- *Mobile:* Hamburger menu opening a full-screen or slide-out drawer.

### Typography Blocks (Content Sections)
- *Structure:* Overline (Small, Uppercase, Tracking wide) + Main Heading (Serif, Large) + Body Paragraph (Sans-serif, Muted).
- *Spacing Rules:*
  - `16px` (`mb-4`) below Overline.
  - `24px` or `32px` (`mb-6` or `mb-8`) below Main Heading.
  - `16px` to `24px` (`space-y-4` or `space-y-5`) between paragraphs.

### Footer
- *Structure:* Dark green/blue background (`bg-green-deep`), centered layout.
- *Elements:* Large italicized quote (Serif), author name, thin gold divider, logo/name, copyright text (small, muted).
- *Spacing Rules:* Huge padding (`py-16`), `32px` (`mb-8`) margin below main quotes.

## STEP 7 — RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The design system is fluid and adapts elegantly across all screen sizes.

### Mobile (`< 768px`)
- **Grid / Layout:** 1-column structure. All content blocks stack vertically (`flex-col`).
- **Typography:**
  - `H1` (Hero) scales down from `72px` to `48px` or `56px` to avoid awkward wrapping.
  - `H2` scales down from `48px` to `36px` or `40px`.
  - Body text remains at `16px` for readability, but line-height is tightened slightly if space is an issue.
- **Spacing:** Section paddings are reduced from `py-24` (96px) to `py-16` (64px) or `py-12` (48px). Horizontal padding is reduced to `px-4` (16px) or `px-6` (24px).
- **Navigation:** Switches to a hidden hamburger menu (`lg:hidden`), opening a full-screen or slide-out overlay.
- **Buttons:** Buttons become full-width (`w-full`) to optimize tap targets on touch devices.

### Tablet (`768px - 1024px`)
- **Grid / Layout:** 2-column structure for features, cards, and pillars.
- **Typography:** Middle ground between mobile and desktop scales. `H1` around `60px`.
- **Spacing:** Moderate vertical section padding, `py-16` or `py-20`.
- **Navigation:** Standard header elements begin to appear, though space constraints might necessitate keeping secondary links hidden.

### Desktop (`> 1024px`)
- **Grid / Layout:** 3-column or 4-column structures are used for metrics, feature grids, and complex layouts.
- **Typography:** Full typographic scale utilized (`H1` = `72px`, `H2` = `48px`).
- **Spacing:** Generous use of whitespace. Maximum section padding `py-24` (96px). Content is constrained by `max-w-6xl` (1152px) or `max-w-4xl` (896px) to maintain readable line lengths (around 60-80 characters per line).
- **Navigation:** Full horizontal navigation menu is visible, along with primary Call to Action buttons.

## STEP 8 — COMPONENT LIBRARY

The library should be structured to allow modular reuse and consistency across tools (Figma, Storybook, React, Webflow).

### Organization Strategy

#### 1. Foundations (Design Tokens)
These are the core variables that control the visual language. They should be stored in Figma styles or a `theme.js` (for Tailwind/React).

- **Colors:** Defined per the token scale (e.g., Primary, Accent, Neutral, Background, Foreground).
- **Typography Styles:** H1, H2, H3, H4, Body Large, Body Base, Caption, Overline.
- **Spacing System:** The 4px/8px incremental scale.
- **Shadows:** Including specific custom shadows like the gold button shadow.
- **Border Radii:** Values for `sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`, and `full`.

#### 2. Elements (Atoms)
The smallest, indivisible components of the interface.

- **Buttons:**
  - `ButtonPrimary` (Gradient gold)
  - `ButtonSecondary` (Outline)
  - `ButtonText` (Transparent)
- **Icons:** A standardized set of SVG icons (using the `lucide` library format), sized at `w-5 h-5` or `w-6 h-6`.
- **Dividers:** Horizontal lines used for structure.
- **Form Inputs:** Basic text inputs and textareas (though limited in current view, should be designed for contact forms).

#### 3. Modules (Molecules)
Combinations of elements working together as a unit.

- **Accordion Item:** Combines a heading, an icon, and hidden body text.
- **Card:** Combines a heading, body text, and padding.
- **Stat Block:** Combines a large number (e.g., "50+") and a small caption (e.g., "Escolas atendidas").

#### 4. Templates (Organisms)
Larger sections of the UI, built from modules and elements.

- **Header / Navigation:** Combines Logo, Navigation Links, and a Primary Button.
- **Hero Section:** Combines H1, Body Large, and CTA buttons on a dark background.
- **Features Section:** Combines an Overline, H2, and a Grid of feature Cards.
- **FAQ Section:** Combines an Overline, H2, and a stack of Accordion Items.
- **Footer:** Combines quotes, links, dividers, and copyright info.

### Workflow Recommendations
- **Figma:** Use Auto Layout extensively to mirror the CSS Flexbox/Grid behavior. Define text and color styles explicitly.
- **React / Next.js:** Build components using Tailwind CSS utility classes, utilizing the `theme.extend` feature in `tailwind.config.js` to map directly to the defined Design Tokens. Use tools like `framer-motion` for accordion transitions.
- **Webflow:** Use the standard CSS variables (`:root`) to define colors and fonts. Create "Symbols" (Components) for repeated elements like the Nav Bar and Footer. Create "Classes" that correspond to the module and element names (e.g., `.button-primary`, `.card-feature`).

## STEP 9 — DESIGN GUIDELINES

To maintain the premium, professional, and accessible feel of Tecer Educacional, developers and designers must adhere to the following rules:

### 1. Consistency Rules
- **Never mix font families arbitrarily.** `Playfair Display` is exclusively for headings, large numbers, and impactful quotes. `Source Sans 3` is for all other text (body, buttons, captions, overlines).
- **Use the spacing scale strictly.** Do not use arbitrary margin or padding values (e.g., `17px`). Always snap to the 4px/8px grid (e.g., `16px`, `24px`).
- **Button usage:** There should only be one primary CTA (`bg-gradient-gold`) visible in a given viewport to draw focus. Secondary actions must use the outline or text button styles.
- **Corner radii:** Keep border radii consistent. If a card uses `12px` (`rounded-xl`), all similar cards in that section must use the same radius.

### 2. Accessibility & Contrast
- **Color Contrast:** All text must pass WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 ratio for normal text, 3:1 for large text).
  - *Example:* The gold accent (`#d49a35`) on the deep dark background (`#111622`) passes comfortably.
  - *Warning:* Be careful using white text on light gold backgrounds; ensure sufficient darkness in gradients.
- **Focus States:** All interactive elements (buttons, links, form inputs) must have a visible focus state for keyboard navigation.
- **Aria Labels:** Ensure accordions and icon-only buttons (like the mobile hamburger menu) have appropriate `aria-expanded` and `aria-label` attributes.
- **Legibility:** Maintain the standard line-height of `1.5` to `1.6` for body text to ensure comfortable long-form reading.

### 3. Typography Usage & Hierarchy
- **Overline Context:** Always use the uppercase, widely-tracked overline (`tracking-[0.2em]`) above main section headings (H2) to provide context (e.g., "Próximo Passo", "Dúvidas Frequentes").
- **Heading Hierarchy:** Do not skip heading levels (e.g., jumping from H2 to H4). This is crucial for both SEO and screen readers.
- **Text Alignment:** Center-align text only for hero sections, footers, or short section introductions. For paragraphs longer than 3 lines, use left-alignment to improve readability.

### 4. Layout Hierarchy
- **Sectioning:** Use alternating background colors (Dark `#111622` vs Light `#ffffff` or `#faf8f5`) to clearly separate major page sections.
- **Breathing Room:** Do not fear whitespace. The `py-24` (96px) padding on desktop is intentional; it creates the "peace to lead" feeling central to the brand's message.
- **Containment:** Keep text line lengths under 80 characters. Use `max-w-4xl` (approx. 896px) for articles or text-heavy single-column layouts to prevent eye strain.
